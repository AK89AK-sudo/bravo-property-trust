The Platform

Bravo Property Trust originates and manages commercial real estate credit with the discipline of an institution and the speed of a principal. Every loan is underwritten to a defined exit, most commonly a HUD-insured permanent financing executed by our affiliate, Bravo Capital, one of the nation’s active HUD MAP and LEAN approved lenders.

Certainty of Execution

As a balance-sheet lender with committed capital partners, we close on the terms we quote, on the timeline sponsors need.

A Defined Exit, By Design

Bridge loans are structured from day one around the permanent takeout, with the HUD path underwritten in parallel by our affiliate platform.

Institutional Underwriting

Credit-first structuring: milestone-gated funding, cash management, and covenant discipline that protects sponsors and capital alike.

Relationship Lending

The Bravo Platform

Credit Discipline

Our record is built on paper that protects capital: structure, visibility, and control from origination through resolution.

i.

Underwrite the exit first

Every bridge loan begins with the permanent financing it will become. Sizing, covenants, and milestones are all built backward from a defined takeout.

ii.

Structure over hope

Milestone-gated advances, controlled cash management, meaningful recourse, and reporting discipline on every transaction, for every sponsor.

iii.

Collateral-first credit

We lend against real assets at conservative attachment points, with third-party valuation and independent diligence on every loan.

iv.

Active asset management

Post-closing, every position is monitored through stabilization with the same rigor it was underwritten, protecting sponsors, investors, and the platform.
