Strategies

Credit across the life of an asset.

Every loan is underwritten to a defined exit, most commonly a HUD-insured permanent financing executed by affiliate Bravo Capital.

i.

Bridge & Construction

First-mortgage bridge and construction financing for multifamily and healthcare assets in acquisition, development, lease-up, or repositioning.

Senior Secured
ii.

Bridge-to-HUD

Interim capital engineered from day one to bridge into a HUD-insured permanent loan, 223(f), 221(d)(4), and 232, processed by our MAP and LEAN approved affiliate.

Defined Exit
iii.

Mezzanine & Structured

Subordinate and structured positions completing the capital stack for experienced sponsors, with aligned, credit-first terms.
